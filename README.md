# 🐾 Pet World: Ferrari Rescue & Dream House 🏎️

> **A high-quality interactive pet adoption & driving adventure game built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Web Audio API. Designed to be played in any browser and installable directly to your iPad or iPhone home screen!**

---

## 🌟 Game Highlights

### 🏎️ 1. The Signature Orange Ferrari
* **High Performance Supercar**: Complete with aerodynamic curves, spoiler wings, gold star rims, and nitro boosters!
* **Co-Pilot Passenger Seat**: Your adopted pet rides right next to you with their ears and paws bobbing in the breeze wearing sunglasses and bandanas!
* **Interactive Driving Highway**: Drive between the **Animal Shelter** and the **Dream Pet House**. Steer, accelerate, jump off stunt ramps, collect golden coins and pet treats, and hit the turbo boost with authentic synthesized engine roars and horns!
* **Ferrari Custom Garage**: Customize paint colors (Sunset Orange, Corsa Red, Blaze Gold, Cyber Magenta), GT wings, RGB underglow neon, and Italian victory fanfares.

### 🐾 2. All 11 Unique Animated Animals
1. 🐶 **Puppies** (Golden Retriever, Corgi, Dalmatian — energetic tail wags & puppy kisses)
2. 🐱 **Cats** (Calico & Tabby — deep purring, acrobatic laser chaser)
3. 🐱 **Kittens** (Tiny fluffballs with giant shiny eyes and squeaky mews)
4. 🦜 **Parrots** (Sun Conure & Macaw — colorful crests, mimicking Ferrari horns)
5. 🦎 **Geckos** (Leopard Gecko — sweet permanent smiles & sticky toe climbs)
6. 🦡 **Ferret** (Masked noodle bandit — high-speed ferret war dances & tunnel zooms)
7. 🦎 **Axolotl** (Pink & Golden water dragons with feathery coral gills & water bloops)
8. 🦔 **Hedgehog** (Pygmy apricot — soft peach bellies, curling into cute spike balls)
9. 🐍 **Snake** (Friendly pastel rainbow python wearing miniature hats)
10. 🐹 **Hamsters** (Roborovski dwarf — chubby cheek pouchers & wheel runners)
11. 🐹 **Guinea Pigs** (Abyssinian fluff-potatoes — loud "WHEEK WHEEK" whistles & popcorn hops)

### 🏥 3. Sunshine Animal Rescue Shelter
* **Adoption Registry**: Browse bios, personality traits, and favorite treats for all animals.
* **Interactive Vet Clinic**: Stethoscope checkups, gentle bandages, and rainbow vitamin tonics.
* **Bubble Grooming Spa**: Warm bubbly suds, silky brushes, and warm air fluffer.
* **Official Adoption Ceremony**: Customize your pet's name and receive an official **Adoption Certificate** complete with a gold wax seal!

### 🏡 4. The Dream Pet House
* **5 Interactive Rooms**:
  * 🛋️ **Living Room**: Comfy sofas, castle cat tree, and crystal water palace.
  * 🌳 **Backyard Playground**: Agility tunnels, swimming splash pool, and sun decks.
  * 🍽️ **Gourmet Kitchen**: Treat buffet islands and snack mixers.
  * 🛁 **Pet Spa & Bath**: Jacuzzi bubbles and grooming vanity mirror.
  * 🛏️ **Cozy Bedroom**: Canopy beds and soothing night lullabies.
* **Play with Toys**: Throw tennis balls and control dancing laser pointers!
* **Dress-Up Wardrobe**: Equip party hats, golden crowns, detective caps, aviator goggles, heart glasses, satin bowties, Ferrari racing bandanas, and superhero capes.
* **Photo Booth & Album**: Snap polaroids with custom stickers and save memories to your photo album.

### 🏆 5. Arcade Mini-Games
* 🦴 **Treat Catcher**: Catch falling snacks in your pet's bowl.
* ⚡ **Agility Hurdle Course**: Time jumps over obstacles, fiery rings, and tunnels.
* 👑 **Fashion Runway Show**: Strike glamorous poses for judges and win championship trophies.

## 📱 How to Install on iPad / iPhone (Zero App Store Needed!)

1. Open the game in **Safari** on your iPad or iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up 📤).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** ➕.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. **Done!** The game icon will appear on your iPad home screen and launch in full-screen standalone mode with offline support!

## 💻 Development & Local Preview

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start local development server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build

# Preview production build
npm run preview
```

## 🚀 Publishing to GitHub Pages

1. Push this repository to your GitHub account: `https://github.com/olstevens02-coder`
2. Go to your repository **Settings** ➔ **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch** (choose `main` or `gh-pages` and `/dist` or root).
4. Your game will be live on the web at `https://olstevens02-coder.github.io/pet-world/`!
